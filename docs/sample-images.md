# Licensed photographs for the sample workspace

The six local assets below were downloaded from original photograph links on **2026-10-31**, decoded, orientation-corrected, JPEG-compressed and stripped of EXIF metadata. They are used in the prepared presentation workspace, with internal origin flags and retained creator/license metadata. No AI-enhanced variants were downloaded.

**These photographs do not verify any Iligan incident, status, repair or office action.** Their source pages do not identify an original locality, so no locality is invented. Sample map pins describe illustrative scenarios inside the chosen barangay; they are not the photographs’ capture coordinates.

All six source pages state **[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)** and require credit to **tOrange.biz**. The manifest records the credited author, original source, license link, caption, exact local file, dimensions, byte size and SHA-256. For the classroom presentation, per-photo credit text is temporarily hidden in resident and staff photo views at the owner's request; source/license metadata remains stored so attribution can be restored later. Restore visible attribution or replace these assets with appropriately licensed photographs before public release. Resizing/compression is recorded in each attribution.
